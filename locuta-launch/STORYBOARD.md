## Video direction

One warm, encouraging world throughout: cream canvas, Locuta green (#3FCE6F) as the single hero accent, Baloo 2 rounded display, capsule pill geometry, soft candy accents. Motion is friendly and springy (ease-out, slight overshoot on pills), never frantic. Real UI (frames 3–5) sits inside pill-outlined device frames so it belongs to the same world. Music starts soft under the hook, opens up at "Meet Locuta" (frame 3), and peaks into the CTA. No narration — text reveals in time with the music's phrasing.
